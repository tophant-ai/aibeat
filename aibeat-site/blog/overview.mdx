The AI Beat blog is where we explain the attacks and evaluation ideas behind the product in depth: how a published attack actually works, how we turn it into a black-box, declarative AI Beat Case, and what the evidence looks like when it runs against a real agent.

Every post is written to be reproducible with the checked-in tooling and to respect a strict rule: we describe mechanisms and show field-level Case examples, never the live payloads, attack goals, or canaries used against a target.

## How posts are structured

- **The attack in one paragraph** — what the published technique does and why it works.
- **As an AI Beat Case** — the declarative fields you set, with a safe, field-level example.
- **How AI Beat judges it** — the pass/fail contract and, where relevant, the two-gate verdict.
- **What the evidence shows** — a redacted view of a real run, so you can tell a true success from a blocked attempt.
